The algorithm

Three steps from Mars to Earth

01

Weight the criteria

Set how important each criterion for similarity is to your mission, from 0 (ignore) to 1 (critical).

02

Pick a Mars target

Click anywhere on the 3D Mars globe or type coordinates. We estimate that spot's profile from nearby orbital data.

03

Explore the analogs

Every tested Earth location is scored per criterion. Weights scale your importance by how well each criterion separates the candidates.

sik = 1 − |eik − mk| wk = fk·vk / Σj fj·vj scorei = Σk wk·sik

f = your importance · v = spread (discriminating power) of each criterion across all tested locations
