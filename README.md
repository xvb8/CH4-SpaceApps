The Mars Analog Finder is a website that analyzes locations Earth and compares them to a chosen location on Mars using geological (basaltic, phyllosilicates/clay, sulfates, carbonates, silica/evaporites), landforms & terrain (topography, aeolian bed forms, caves), climate & radiation (temperature, aridity, water availability), and water (ice, permafrost, salt deliquescnce potential, periglacial ground and gullies) criterion to identify the top 5 locations on Earth that closely resemble areas on Mars on a scale of 0 to 1. The identification of potential analog locations of Mars here on Earth is important because it allows scientists to study and test equipment in environments on Earth that closely replicate conditions on Mars before sending missions into space. For example, researchers could use locations in deserts or volcanic regions to test their technology that may be used during future Mars missions. Overall, the Mars Analog Finder could help ameliorate the safety of future space explorations, make the Mars planning stages more efficient, and allow scientists to understand the challenges of the Martian environment before the mission actually commences.
